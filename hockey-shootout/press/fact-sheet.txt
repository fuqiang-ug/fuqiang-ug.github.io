Pond Hockey Shootout: Ice Game

A hockey shootout made for your wrist. Flick the puck past six family goalies, from the kid to Biscuit the dog, and come back for a new shootout every day.

Flick up the ice with one finger to shoot: the direction of the flick is the aim and its speed is the power. A goal is worth 1 point, a top corner 3, and a one-timer off a pass counts double. Goals in a row multiply your score, up to 5 times. A save, a post or a miss costs a puck, and the third lost puck ends the run. Every three goals the next of six family goalies takes over. Tap the calendar before a run for today's shootout, the same for everyone that day.
