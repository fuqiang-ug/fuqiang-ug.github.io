How to play

Flick up the ice with one finger to shoot: the direction of the flick is the aim and its speed is the power. A goal is worth 1 point, a top corner 3, and a one-timer off a pass counts double. Goals in a row multiply your score, up to 5 times. A save, a post or a miss costs a puck, and the third lost puck ends the run. Every three goals the next of six family goalies takes over. Tap the calendar before a run for today's shootout, the same for everyone that day.

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. You pay once. There are no ads and no purchases inside the game. Outside the US, the price is the one in your country's App Store.

Made for Apple Watch

One finger flicks the puck, haptics tell a save from a goal, and a round takes about a minute. The watch game plays without the iPhone app.

Six goalies, one family

The kid, Dad who always dives the same way, Biscuit the golden retriever who chases the puck, Grandpa who remembers your last goal, the twins and the captain.

Four ponds

A handmade miniature world: a forest in the morning, the mountains in the afternoon, a backyard rink at dusk and a city park at night under string lights.

Life around the pond

A bullfinch on the crossbar, Grandma cheering on the bank, and now and then a squirrel that Biscuit cannot leave alone.

A new shootout every day

Today's shootout is the same for everyone, with its own Game Center leaderboard. There is an all-time board, 10 achievements and 17 missions too.

Prizes on the net

Knock the tin can or the mitten off the net for 2 more points and a lost puck back. In December the can is a Christmas market mug, and at Lunar New Year the mitten is a red envelope.

Widgets and a complication

On the watch face, in the Smart Stack and on the iPhone Home Screen, today's shootout is one tap away.

For everyone

Easier gives a fourth puck, slower goalies and kinder aim. A flick length setting, Reduce Motion and VoiceOver are supported.

No strings

No ads, no accounts and no purchases inside the game. Scores go to Game Center only when you are signed in.

Getting it on your Apple Watch

  1. Install the app on your iPhone first.
  2. Open the Watch app on your iPhone.
  3. Tap My Watch, then tap Apps.
  4. Scroll down to Available Apps and tap Install next to Pond Hockey.

Or install it on the watch itself: open the App Store on Apple Watch, search for Pond Hockey and tap the price. An Apple Watch set up for a child gets its apps this way.

Most of the time you can skip these steps. Apple Watch installs the watch version of your iPhone apps by itself unless that setting is turned off, so the game usually appears on your watch a few minutes after it lands on your iPhone.

Questions

Does it work without an iPhone?

Yes, the Apple Watch game plays without the iPhone app, and you can install it from the App Store on Apple Watch. Apple Watch itself is set up with an iPhone, a parent's for a child's watch.

Can I play on iPhone alone?

Yes. The iPhone game is the same shootout, flicked with one finger. An Apple Watch is not required.

Is there a free version?

No. You pay once, and there are no ads and no purchases inside the game.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game plays offline. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to it when there is a connection.

Does it collect any data?

No. The game has no account of ours, no analytics and no advertising. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center; nothing is sent to us.

Will it send me notifications?

Only if you turn reminders on. Then it sends at most two, when you have not played for a few days. You can switch them off under Help.

Is there sound?

On iPhone, yes: sound effects and a winter music loop. They stay quiet in silent mode, and the music can be switched off under Help. On Apple Watch the game uses haptics.

Which languages is it in?

English, Japanese, German, French, Korean and Traditional Chinese.

How do I get it on my Apple Watch?

It usually installs by itself after you install the iPhone app. If it does not: Open the Watch app on your iPhone. Tap My Watch, then tap Apps. Scroll down to Available Apps and tap Install next to Pond Hockey. Or install it on the watch itself: open the App Store on Apple Watch, search for Pond Hockey and tap the price. An Apple Watch set up for a child gets its apps this way.
