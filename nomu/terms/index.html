What Nomu is

Nomu describes café drinks and lets you show a card of the drink and customizations you want. Nomu does not place, send, or pay for orders, and is not connected to any café.

Customizations are requests; availability depends on the café. Drink descriptions are general information about how cafés commonly make a drink and may differ from what a café serves.

Nomu does not provide nutrition, allergen, caffeine, or certification information. Do not rely on the app for dietary or health decisions — ask the café.

Nomu+

Nomu+ is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or annual) that adds more drinks. The price is shown in the store before you buy. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You can cancel anytime from Settings, which opens the store's subscription management.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Content updates

Drink information may be updated, and individual drinks may be added, changed, or removed.
