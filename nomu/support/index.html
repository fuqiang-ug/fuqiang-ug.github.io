Frequently asked questions

How does the order card work?

Pick a drink, adjust it with compatible customizations, and Nomu turns your choice into a clean card you show at the counter. Nomu never places, sends, or pays for an order and is not affiliated with any café — customizations are requests, and the café decides.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Nomu is anonymous — no account, no sign-in, no email required. Your favorites, recent searches, customizations, and language settings are stored on your device and never uploaded. There is no advertising and no cross-app tracking. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

How do I cancel Nomu+?

In the app, open Settings → Nomu+ → Manage or cancel subscription, which opens your store’s subscription screen. Or directly on your device: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nomu+ → Cancel (or Google Play → Subscriptions). You keep Nomu+ until the end of your current billing period.

What is Nomu+?

The core app is complete without paying — discover, customize, save favorites, and show the card. Nomu+ is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or annual) that unlocks the full drink catalog. The price is shown in the store before you buy, and it renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I found a bug.

Email fuqiangug@gmail.com with a short description of what happened and your device model. A screenshot helps. We’ll take a look.

How do I delete my data?

Nomu has no accounts and stores nothing about you on our servers. To erase everything, uninstall the app — uninstalling removes all local data.
