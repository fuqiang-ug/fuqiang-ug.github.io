Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Nomu is a café drink guide. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it stays.

No account

Nomu has no account and no sign-in. Your favorites, recent searches, selected customizations, and language settings are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded or synced.

Anonymous usage data

To understand how the app is used, Nomu may send anonymous usage events — such as which drink was opened, how many customizations a card has, and which app and card languages are in use. These events contain no name, email address, contact details, or search text, and are not used for advertising.

Nomu contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Nomu does not request your location.

Purchases

Nomu+ is billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app uses RevenueCat to confirm whether your subscription is active. Nomu never receives your payment card details.

What we explicitly do not collect

  • No email, name, phone number, or physical address (there are no accounts).
  • No device location (no location permission is requested).
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers; no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No search text leaves your device.
  • No bank or payment-card data — Nomu is never in the payment path.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all information Nomu stored on your device.

Your rights

Because Nomu holds no personal information about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf — all of your data lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Subscription and receipt records held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat are governed by those companies' own privacy practices and data-request flows.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected in the in-app policy and at this hosted URL.
