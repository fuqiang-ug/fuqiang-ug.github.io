Owed — find what you're owed.

Class action settlements, government rebates, and consumer redress programs return billions of dollars to consumers every year. Most eligible people never claim what they're owed — they don't know the settlement exists, they don't know if they qualify, or they miss the deadline.

Owed surfaces relevant settlements to you, tells you whether you might qualify, tracks deadlines, and links you to the official claim form. We don't file claims on your behalf; we make it possible for you to do it yourself.

Built on court records, not guesswork

Every settlement in Owed is cross-verified against the original court filings and administrator sites. We show you the eligibility criteria, the estimated payout range, the deadline, and a direct link to where you actually claim.
