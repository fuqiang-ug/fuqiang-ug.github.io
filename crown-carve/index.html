How to play

Steer left and right between trees, rocks, logs and snowmen: turn the Digital Crown on Apple Watch, or drag on iPhone. The further you go, the faster it gets, and one crash ends the run. A pass close to an obstacle is worth 10 points and a ramp is worth 30. Skiing between two flags multiplies everything you score, up to 5 times, until the multiplier runs out. A blue shield takes one crash for you.

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. You pay once. There are no ads and no purchases inside the game. Outside the US, the price is the one in your country's App Store.

Steer with the Digital Crown

Turn the crown to carve on Apple Watch. On iPhone, drag left and right.

A mountain that changes

Forest, snow park and icy ridge, from daylight through dusk into night.

Ramps, gates and ice

Ramps throw you over the next rows. Flag gates multiply your score up to five times. On ice you can barely steer.

Runs of a minute or two

One crash ends the run. The next one starts with a turn of the crown.

Seven skiers to unlock

The distance you ski across all your runs unlocks them.

No strings

No ads, no accounts and no purchases inside the game. Nothing you do in the game leaves your devices.

Getting it on your Apple Watch

  1. Install the app on your iPhone first.
  2. Open the Watch app on your iPhone.
  3. Tap My Watch, then tap Apps.
  4. Scroll down to Available Apps and tap Install next to Crown Carve.

Most of the time you can skip these steps. Apple Watch installs the watch version of your iPhone apps by itself unless that setting is turned off, so the game usually appears on your watch a few minutes after it lands on your iPhone.

Questions

Does it work without an iPhone?

You need an iPhone to install it. Once the game is on your Apple Watch, it plays without the iPhone nearby.

Can I play on iPhone alone?

Yes. The iPhone game is the same run, steered by dragging. An Apple Watch is not required.

Is there a free version?

No. You pay once, and there are no ads and no purchases inside the game.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game never connects to the internet.

Does it collect any data?

No. The game has no account, no analytics and no advertising. Your scores stay on your devices.

Will it send me notifications?

Only if you turn reminders on. Then it sends at most two, when you have not skied for a few days. You can switch them off under Help in the iPhone app.

Which languages is it in?

English, Japanese, German, French, Korean and Traditional Chinese.

How do I get it on my Apple Watch?

It usually installs by itself after you install the iPhone app. If it does not: Open the Watch app on your iPhone. Tap My Watch, then tap Apps. Scroll down to Available Apps and tap Install next to Crown Carve.
