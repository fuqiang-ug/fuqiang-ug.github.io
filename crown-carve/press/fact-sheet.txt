Crown Carve: Ski Game

A downhill ski run for Apple Watch and iPhone. Turn the Digital Crown, carve through the trees, and see how far you get before the mountain wins.

Steer left and right between trees, rocks, logs and snowmen: turn the Digital Crown on Apple Watch, or drag on iPhone. The further you go, the faster it gets, and one crash ends the run. A pass close to an obstacle is worth 10 points and a ramp is worth 30. Skiing between two flags multiplies everything you score, up to 5 times, until the multiplier runs out. A blue shield takes one crash for you.

Plays on: iPhone and Apple Watch
Requires: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.
Price: US$1.99. You pay once. There are no ads and no purchases inside the game. Outside the US, the price is the one in your country's App Store.
