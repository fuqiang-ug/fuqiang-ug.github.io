Crown Carve: Ski Game

A hand-painted trail map comes alive on your wrist. Turn the Digital Crown, carve through the gates and the forest, and ski today's mountain against everyone else.

Steer left and right between trees, rocks, logs and snowmen: turn the Digital Crown on Apple Watch, or drag on iPhone. The further you go, the faster it gets, and one crash ends the run. A pass close to an obstacle is worth 10 points and a ramp is worth 30. Skiing between two flags multiplies everything you score, up to 5 times, until the multiplier runs out. A blue shield takes one crash for you. Tap the calendar between runs for today's mountain, the same slope for everyone that day.

Plays on: iPhone and Apple Watch
Requires: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.
