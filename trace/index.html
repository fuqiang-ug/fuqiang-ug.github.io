What trace does not do

A tool, not a verdict.

Trace is a private journal for adults — not a medical app and not an AI product. Some things are deliberately out of scope.

No AI

No scans or analysis

No face scan, no symmetry or skin analysis, no photo enhancement, no generated transformations. Your photos are never analysed.

No score

It never rates you

No attractiveness score, no "potential", no rank. trace shows your own numbers and lets you judge for yourself.

No advice

Not a medical app

trace records what you log. It never suggests amounts, schedules or products, and gives no diagnosis, treatment or dosing guidance.

No noise

No streaks or urgency

No invented streak metric, no countdowns, no shame. Skipped days are just data, and reminders stay neutral.
