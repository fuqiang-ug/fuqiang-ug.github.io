Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Fuqiang Pipeline Test collects no data. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not connect to the internet.

What the app collects

Nothing. Fuqiang Pipeline Test has no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The app does not connect to the internet. Nothing you do in it leaves your device, and we never see it.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or notifications.

Apple

You get the app through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the download, and any payment, under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal details about you from Apple.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking tools. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host's servers process the IP address of each visitor in order to deliver the page.

If you email us

If you write to fuqiangug@gmail.com, we use your address and your message to answer you, and we keep the correspondence only as long as that takes.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask for access to personal data we hold about you, and for its correction, deletion or restriction. You can also object to its use and ask for a copy in a portable format. Because the app collects nothing, the only personal data we could hold is an email you sent us.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.
