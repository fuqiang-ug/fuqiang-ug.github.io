Fuqiang Pipeline Test

A one-tap reflex game used to test our release process.

Tap the orange dot to score a point. The dot jumps to a new spot after every tap. There is no timer and no way to lose, so a round lasts as long as you like.

Plays on: iPhone and Apple Watch
Requires: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.
Price: Free. No ads and no purchases inside the app.
Version: 1.0
Released: Not released yet
