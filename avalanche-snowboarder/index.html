How to play

Hold anywhere on the screen to dive down a hill and gather speed. Let go on the rise to fly, and come down along the next slope to keep your speed and multiply your score, up to five times. While you float, the rider does tricks; a clean landing keeps their points and fills the SHRED meter. Snowflakes are worth 5 points, ice patches and gates fire you forward, and each lift station pushes the avalanche back and opens the next island. The run ends when the avalanche catches you.

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. You pay once. There are no ads and no purchases inside the game. Outside the US, the price is the one in your country's App Store.

One finger

Hold on the way down, let go on the way up. That is the whole control, on Apple Watch and on iPhone.

Tricks and SHRED

While you float, the rider throws grabs, spins and flips. Fill the meter and SHRED doubles every point for eight seconds.

Ten paper islands

Each island has its own light, trees, mountains and a landmark, from dawn to the northern lights.

A new mountain every day

The daily run is the same mountain for everyone, with its own Game Center leaderboard.

Made for the wrist

You feel the avalanche before you see it, as taps that come faster. The Watch app also plays without the iPhone app.

No strings

No ads, no accounts and no purchases inside the game. Only Game Center scores leave your devices, and only if you are signed in to it.

Getting it on your Apple Watch

  1. Install the app on your iPhone first.
  2. Open the Watch app on your iPhone.
  3. Tap My Watch, then tap Apps.
  4. Scroll down to Available Apps and tap Install next to Avalanche.

Most of the time you can skip these steps. Apple Watch installs the watch version of your iPhone apps by itself unless that setting is turned off, so the game usually appears on your watch a few minutes after it lands on your iPhone.

Questions

Does it work without an iPhone?

Yes. You can install it from the App Store on Apple Watch, and it plays there without the iPhone app.

Can I play on iPhone alone?

Yes. The iPhone game is the same run, played with the phone held sideways. An Apple Watch is not required.

Is there a free version?

No. You pay once, and there are no ads and no purchases inside the game.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Only the Game Center leaderboards and achievements need one.

Does it collect any data?

No. The game has no account of its own, no analytics and no advertising. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center.

Will it send me notifications?

Only if you turn reminders on. Then it sends at most two, when you have not played for a few days. You can switch them off under Help in the iPhone app.

Which languages is it in?

English, Japanese, German, French, Korean and Traditional Chinese.

How do I get it on my Apple Watch?

It usually installs by itself after you install the iPhone app. If it does not: Open the Watch app on your iPhone. Tap My Watch, then tap Apps. Scroll down to Available Apps and tap Install next to Avalanche.
